Executive summary

Card payments through the public API failed for 47 minutes on 14 September, from 09:12 to 09:59 UTC. Checkout requests returned errors for roughly one in three attempts; no payment data was lost and no duplicate charges were created. The direct cause was a configuration change that reduced the connection pool for the payments service. The decision required from the reader is whether to fund a staged configuration rollout in the next planning cycle.

Impact

Approximately one in three checkout attempts failed during the window. Support received 84 tickets, all answered within the same day. Three merchants reported abandoned carts; none reported duplicate charges. Internal dashboards were accurate throughout.

Root cause

A configuration change reduced the payments service connection pool from 40 to 8 connections to address an unrelated warning. Under the morning traffic profile this exhausted the pool and produced timeouts. The change passed review because the review checklist does not ask for a traffic estimate.

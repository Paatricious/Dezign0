Questions freelancers ask before switching

Can my clients pay without an account?

Yes. Every invoice has a payment page with card and bank transfer options, and no sign-up is required.

What happens if an invoice is late?

Ledgerly sends three polite reminders on a schedule you set, then tells you to call the client yourself.

Do you support multiple currencies?

Invoices can be issued in your client's currency, and the exchange rate is recorded on the invoice date.

Can I export my records for tax time?

Download a CSV of paid and open invoices for any date range, ready for your accountant.
